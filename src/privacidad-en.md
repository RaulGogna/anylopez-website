## 6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights before the controller:

- **Access** to your personal data.
- **Rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") when the data is no longer necessary.
- **Restriction** of processing in the cases set out in art. 18 GDPR.
- **Portability** of your data in a structured format (where the basis is consent or contract).
- **Objection** to processing where the basis is legitimate interest.
- **Withdrawal of consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise your rights, write to **gognagroup@gmail.com** stating your name, the right you wish to exercise and documentation proving your identity. The maximum response period is one month (extendable by a further two months in complex cases).

If you consider that the processing does not comply with the GDPR, you may lodge a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency** (AEPD) at [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).
